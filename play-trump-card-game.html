Four ways to play

1. Solo against bots

Press the big button and you're the only human at a table of four — your three opponents are patient, legal-playing bots. It's the fastest way to learn, and a built-in coach walks you through naming trump, following suit, and trumping for the first time. No rule book required.

2. Hot-seat with family (one screen)

Perfect for game night on one tablet or phone. Choose which seats are human and pass the device around. Only the current player's hand is shown, so partners never see each other's cards.

3. With friends online (private room)

Host a game and you instantly get a four-letter room code and a share link. Send it to your partner (or the whole table) and they jump straight in — no accounts, no friend lists. Empty seats fill with bots, so the game starts while people are still joining.

4. Hot-seat on one phone with a friend

Only two of you? Leave two seats as bots and hot-seat the humans, or go online so you and a friend can sit opposite each other as partners from your own devices.

Frequently asked questions

Is playing Trump here really free?

Yes. No paywall, no coins, no ads blocking your table. It's a small independent project.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser on phone, tablet, or computer. You can add it to your home screen to play like an app.

Can two friends play together online?

Yes. One of you hosts a room (you get a code and invite link), the other joins. The two empty seats fill with bots — sit across from each other as partners.

Is this the same as Top Trumps?

No. This is the classic four-player partnership game where one suit is named trump and beats every other suit. Top Trumps is a different picture-card game.
