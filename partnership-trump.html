Partnership Trump Card Game

Partnership Trump is the four-player team version of Trump: you and a partner sit opposite each other and take on another pair over thirteen tricks. Two fixed teams of two make every hand a team effort — and that's what makes it so good to play with family and friends.

What makes it a partnership game

In most card games you play for yourself. In Partnership Trump, your partner sits directly across from you and your team scores together — first to 7 tricks wins the round, whether the tricks came from you or your partner. You never see each other's hands, so you play from memory, inference, and a little trust.

Partnership rules at a glance

Players4, in two fixed teams of 2; partners sit opposite each other
DeckOne standard 52-card deck (no jokers); Ace high, 2 low
Deal13 cards to each player, one at a time, clockwise
TrumpPlayer 1 names a suit as trump from their first five cards, mid-deal
LeadDealer leads the first trick; the winner of each trick leads the next
GoalYour team takes 7 or more of the 13 tricks to win the round

How a partnership round goes

  1. Deal: thirteen cards each, so two teammates together control half the deck.
  2. Name trump: Player 1 studies their first five cards and names a suit as trump — it outranks every other suit, even a 2 over an Ace.
  3. Play the tricks: follow suit if you can; when void, you may play anything, including trump.
  4. Win the round: the first team to 7 tricks takes it, then the deal rotates left.
Teamwork is the edge. In partnership play you don't just manage your own hand — you watch what your partner signals by winning, losing, and discarding. Leading a suit your partner is strong in, and holding back to not cut them, is where a good pair wins games.

Partnership Trump FAQ

How many people do you need?

Exactly four — you, your partner, and two opponents. On Sundial, empty seats are filled by bots, so you can start with just you and a friend.

Is Partnership Trump the same as regular Trump?

Yes — the rules are identical. "Partnership Trump" is just the name used when the game is played in fixed teams of two, which is how it's most commonly played.

Is it the same as Top Trumps?

No. Top Trumps is a picture-card game. This is the classic four-player trick-taking game where one suit is named trump and beats every other suit.

Can I play with one friend?

Yes. You and a friend can sit across from each other as partners — Sundial fills the two empty seats with bots.
