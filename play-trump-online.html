Three ways to play

1. Play solo against bots

Press the big button and you're in a game as the only human at a table of four — your three opponents are patient, legal-playing bots. It's the fastest way to learn, and the built-in coach walks you through naming trump, following suit, and trumping for the first time (learn by playing, no rule book required).

2. Hot-seat with family (one screen)

Perfect for game night on a single tablet or phone. Choose which seats are human and pass the device around. Player hands stay hidden until it's that player's turn, so partners never see each other's cards.

3. Play with friends online (host a private room)

Host a game and you instantly get a short, four-letter room code plus a share link. Send it to your partner (or the whole table) and they jump straight in — no accounts, no friend lists, no setup. Empty seats are filled by bots, so a game starts even while people are still joining.

Frequently asked questions

Is playing Trump here really free?

Yes. No paywall, no coins, no ads blocking your table. It's a small independent project.

Do I need to download anything?

No. It runs in any modern browser on a phone, tablet, or computer. You can add it to your home screen to play like an app.

Can two friends play Trump online together?

Yes. One of you hosts a room (you get a room code and invite link), the other joins by the link or code. The two remaining seats fill with bots — you and a friend can even sit across from each other as partners.

Can I pause and come back?

Yes. Local games auto-save after every move and resume exactly where you left off, even days later.

Is this the same as Top Trumps or the card game "Trumps"?

No. This is the classic four-player partnership game called Trump (known as Partnership Trump in teams), where one suit is named trump and beats every other suit. It's different from Top Trumps (the picture-card game) and from the invented "Trumps" variant with hidden trump declarations.
