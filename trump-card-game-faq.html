The essentials

How many players do you need for Trump?

Exactly four — two teams of two. Your partner sits directly across from you, and you never see each other's hands.

What is a trump suit?

The trump suit outranks every other suit for the round. A 2 of trump beats an Ace of any non-trump suit. Trump only matters once it's named (before the first trick).

How is trump chosen?

Player 1 — the player to the dealer's left — looks at their first five cards and names one of the four suits as trump. They can't pass, and they can't change it after seeing the rest of their hand.

Do you have to follow suit in Trump?

Yes. If you hold a card of the led suit, you must play one. If you're void in the led suit, you may play anything — including trump.

How many tricks do you need to win a round?

Seven. There are 13 tricks in a round, so 7 or more wins it. Because 13 is odd, a round can never tie 6–6.

Rules & strategy

Can a low card really beat an Ace?

Yes — if it's trump. A 2 of trump beats an Ace of any other suit. That's the whole point of naming trump well.

What happens if you can't follow suit?

You're "void." You may play any card from your hand — a trump to try to win the trick, or a discard to save a card for later.

Who leads the first trick?

The dealer leads the first trick of the round. After that, the winner of each trick leads the next one.

What's the difference between a round and a match?

A round is all 13 tricks; the team with 7 or more wins it. A match goes to the first team to win 3 rounds.

What's the best suit to name as trump?

The one you actually hold. Your first five cards decide the whole round, so name your strongest suit there — ideally two or three cards, especially with an honor.

Should you try to trump every trick?

No. Trump is limited — 13 in the whole deck — and trumping your partner's winning trick wastes it. Save trump for tricks the opponents are otherwise winning.
