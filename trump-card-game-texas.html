Trump Card Game (Texas)

In Texas, Trump is a table game — the four-player, two-team trick-taker that gets played at game nights, card clubs, and around the kitchen table long after the neighbors have gone home. Same Whist-and-Euchre DNA as the rest of the family, but with one signature Texas rule: Player 1 names trump from their first five cards, mid-deal, before seeing the rest of the hand.

Why the five-card trump rule is the whole game

Most trick-taking games deal the whole hand and then set trump. Not this one. Before anyone has their full 13 cards, the player to the dealer's left looks at their first five cards and names a suit as trump. That's a blind bet: you commit to a suit before the rest of your hand arrives. Good calls win rounds; miscalculations let the other team run you over. It rewards judgment, card-watching, and nerve — which is why Texans who grew up on it won't play the tamer versions.

Texas rules at a glance

Players4 — you and your partner vs. the other pair; partners sit opposite
DeckStandard 52-card deck, no jokers; Ace high, 2 low
TrumpNamed by Player 1 from their first five cards; it beats every other suit, even a 2 over an Ace
LeadDealer leads the first trick; the winner of each trick leads the next
Follow suitMandatory if you hold a card of the led suit; when void, play anything (including trump)
WinFirst team to 7 of the 13 tricks wins the round; rotate the deal left
MatchFirst team to win 3 rounds takes the match

FAQs about playing Trump

What's the difference between Trump and Euchre or Whist?

All three are partnership trick-takers. Trump's signature is naming trump mid-deal from your first five cards — no bidding, no turned-up card. Euchre uses a reduced deck and a table of potential trumps; Whist merely turns up the last card.

How do you win a round of Trump?

Take 7 or more of the 13 tricks. Because 13 is odd, a round never ends 6–6 — there's always a winning team.

Can a low card really beat an Ace?

Yes — if it's trump. A 2 of trump beats an Ace of any other suit. That's the whole point of naming trump well.
